# Content Plan: Daily Money Lessons

**Channel promise:** One money lesson a day that makes you smarter about earning, saving, investing and the business world.
**Audience:** Global first (US, UK, Canada, Australia, plus English-speaking India). Viewers in the first four pay the highest ad rates.
**Language:** English. **Currency:** US dollars by default; ₹ only for India-specific stories.
**Examples:** Mostly global companies and universal concepts. About 1 in 5 videos is an India story told for a global viewer (UPI, Zerodha, Amul, IPL), because they're surprising to everyone.
**Format:** YouTube Shorts, 35–55 seconds.
**Upload times:** 6:30 AM IST (8 PM US Eastern / 5 PM Pacific, US prime time) and 10:30 PM IST (5 PM UK / 6 PM Europe / noon US Eastern). We'll adjust after 2–3 weeks of analytics.

> Topic note: topics below written with Indian products (SIP, CIBIL, FD) get a global version by default
> (index fund, credit score, savings account / CD), and keep the Indian version for India-story slots.

## Quality rules (every video)

1. **Every number is either calculated by code or comes from a named source**, and sources go in the description.
2. **No stock tips, no "buy this", no get-rich-quick claims.** We teach how things work.
3. **Disclaimer on screen** whenever returns or investments are shown.
4. **Hook in the first 2 seconds**: a surprising number, a question or a contrast.
5. **No two videos in a row from the same pillar,** and no reused templates back to back. Variety keeps us clear of YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules.
6. **News videos are researched fresh** on the day they're made, with dates checked.
