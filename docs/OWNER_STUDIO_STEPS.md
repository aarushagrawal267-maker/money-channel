# Your one-time YouTube Studio steps (when you're back)

These come from the specialist reviews (23 Nov 2026). Everything else runs automatically.
Do them on a computer at **studio.youtube.com**. Each step is independent; do the top ones first.

## 1. Give Claude more permissions (10 min) — most useful
This lets Claude create playlists, pin comments, add captions and fix titles by itself, so you don't have to.
1. On your computer, download the new `tools/get_youtube_token.py` (Claude will send it).
2. Run it exactly like last time and click **Allow** on every screen (pick the **Penny to Billion** channel).
3. It prints 3 values. Replace the old `YT_REFRESH_TOKEN` in your Claude environment settings with the new one
   (the other two stay the same). Never paste them in chat.

## 2. Block scam comments (5 min)
Finance channels get fake "contact my advisor on WhatsApp" comments that scam viewers.
1. **Settings → Community → Automated filters**.
2. **Blocked words:** paste `whatsapp, telegram, wa.me, t.me, investment manager, account manager, crypto expert, signal app`
3. **Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review:** turn on, choose **Increase strictness**. **Save**.

## 3. Auto-dubbing (2 min)
Your titles are already translated into Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, German and French.
1. **Settings → Upload defaults → Advanced settings**.
2. Make sure **Allow automatic dubbing** is ticked. **Save**.

## 4. Channel home page (10 min, after Costco is live)
1. **Customization → Layout**.
2. **Channel trailer for people who haven't subscribed → Add** → choose **How Costco Makes Money**.
3. **Featured sections → Add section:** Popular videos, then Shorts. **Publish**.

## 5. Weekly, optional (10 min/week)
- **Link each Short to its long video:** Content → Shorts → pencil on the Short → **Related video** → pick the long
  video → Save. (Claude's weekly note will list the pairs.)
- **End screens on long videos:** Content → video → **End screen** → **Import from video** (after the first one) →
  "Best for viewer" + Subscribe → Save. The last 16 seconds of every long video are kept free for this.
- **Thumbnail test:** on a long video, Details → thumbnail → **Test & compare** with a second thumbnail Claude sends.

Skip any step if you're busy; the channel keeps running without them.
