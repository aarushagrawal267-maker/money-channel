# Thumbnail + video look: brand review (25 Nov 2026)

**Verdict:** Long **A**, with one idea taken from C. Shorts **A**. Both fit the banner (navy, gold number, green label, Inter Black), match the inside of the videos, and stay simple.

## Assessment
- **A** looks the most like the brand. It uses the same navy grid, green pill and gold number as the banner and the video scenes, so a viewer who clicks sees the same look inside. In a grid of 10+ videos it gives a calm navy wall where the gold numbers stand out, and the photos keep the videos from looking the same.
- **B** is the most eye-catching, and the yellow does match the coin. But a grid of B would be a wall of yellow, and the coral version for losses breaks the series. That is the "too fancy" problem again. Don't use it.
- **C** looks more expensive with the full-bleed photo, but the photo takes over and the series look gets weaker. The maroon wash and the red bottom strip are off-brand.
- **Gold vs red:** red for losses is the right meaning. Keep it on the number and the chart line only. The background should always stay navy.
- **Video look:** clean and on-brand. The 'side' and 'full' layouts are the strongest.

## Tweaks (priority order)
1. **Keep navy for losses.** Drop the maroon background wash on inflation-type thumbnails. Only the number and the arrow should be red.
2. **Fix the person cut-out edge.** The green glow halo shows rough edges and looks like a sticker. Use a clean mask with a soft dark shadow.
3. **Company photos: drop the white-framed tilted card.** The white frame is the brightest thing on the thumbnail and pulls the eye away from the number. Use C's full-bleed photo fading into navy on the right, or a straight card with a thin border like the ones in the videos. This applies to Shorts A too.
4. **Mobile test.** Keep the number at least 40% of the width and use 4 words or fewer below it. Check every thumbnail at 168x94 px. The pill is too small to read at that size, so make it about 20% bigger and keep it green for every pillar.
5. **Chapter card:** the faint "02" behind the title clashes with it. Lower its opacity or move it to the side.
6. **Count-up underline** touches the comma in "$5,000". Add 8-10 px of space below the number.

## Avoid
- Yellow or coral colour blocks, red backgrounds, and black outlines
- Arrows or circles drawn onto photos, emojis, and shocked faces
- A logo stamp on the thumbnail (YouTube already shows the avatar)
- More than two text colours on one thumbnail
- Photos of real people without a licence that allows it. Credit them in the description, and never suggest the person endorses the channel.
