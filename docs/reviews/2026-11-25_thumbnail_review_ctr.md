# Thumbnail review (CTR), 25 Nov 2026

## Ranking: long videos
1. **A Spotlight**: the best system. It matches the in-video look (navy, gold numbers, green accent), so viewers who know the channel will recognise it. The green rim glow makes the face cut-out stand out at phone size. Its weak spot is the Blockbuster row: the photo is a small tilted card and is hard to read.
2. **C Cinematic**: the strongest for company or place photos, because the full-bleed Blockbuster sign is the hook. For people it adds nothing over A. The thin bottom line can't be seen at phone size.
3. **B Colour block**: it stands out most in the grid, but the panel squeezes the text and the photo. The coral version for inflation breaks consistency. It looks more like a template and less premium for finance.

**Use A, with C's full-bleed treatment whenever the subject is a company or place.** Test B against it with Studio's "Test & Compare" on 2–3 videos, then decide from the data.

## Ranking: Shorts
1. **A**: the number sits high and in the centre. That area stays visible in the Shorts shelf, where the bottom of the image is covered by the title. It also matches the long-video style.
2. **B**: the face and the number fight for attention, and the lower panel gets covered by the shelf title.

## Tweaks, in order of expected impact
1. **Shorten the subline to 3 words or fewer.** Examples: "THEY SAID NO" instead of "THE DEAL THEY REJECTED", and "GONE" instead of "YOUR CASH IS SHRINKING". Let the title carry the context.
2. **Remove the pillar pill** (FOUNDER STORY and the others). It can't be read at phone size and takes up space. Brand recognition can come from the layout and colours.
3. **Make the subject bigger.** A face should fill about 50% of the width, with the eyes in the upper third. Company photos should be full-bleed, never a small card.
4. **Keep the bottom-right 20% clear.** YouTube's duration badge covers the inflation arrow tip and the Blockbuster card there.
5. **Brighten the background for dark-mode feeds.** Lift the navy slightly, put a radial light behind the subject, and use the green glow on every subject.
6. **Make the no-photo design more concrete.** The faint bars should go and the arrow should be thicker. Better still, show a real object, such as a shrinking dollar bill.

## Risks
- **Misleading numbers.** Every number must appear in the video and be sourced. As it stands, "-$360" has no context: it needs a base amount and a time period in the video. Otherwise it breaks YouTube's misleading-thumbnail policy and our own rules.
- **Endorsement and credit.** A real person's face must never suggest they endorse the channel. CC BY photos need a credit in the description.
- **Get-rich-quick look.** "$1.2B" next to a smiling face is fine as a founder story. Never add "YOU CAN TOO"-style lines.
- **Sameness.** Use the same template on every video, but vary the subject and the accent colour, or viewers will scroll past without noticing.
- **Dark-on-dark.** A navy thumbnail can blend into YouTube's dark mode, which most mobile viewers use. Tweak 5 is the fix.
