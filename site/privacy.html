Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy covers "money-channel uploader", a private tool used only by the owner of the Money Mechanics YouTube channel to publish its own videos.

What the tool accesses

  • Permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the Money Mechanics YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel's video statistics and YouTube Analytics, used to choose good upload times.

What the tool does not do

  • It is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's Google or YouTube account.
  • It does not collect, sell or share personal information about viewers or anyone else.
  • It does not use advertising trackers.

How data is stored

The tool's login credentials are stored privately by the channel owner and are used only to upload the channel's own videos and read its own statistics. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
